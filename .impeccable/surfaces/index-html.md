# Surface: studio home (index.html)

Scope: the single page at logicandmatter.com. Visitor mode: Persuade (light). Audience: product users checking who makes a product; they should come away believing a real person stands behind it, then go on to the product, a service, or email.
Constraints: one static HTML file, no build, no JS required, loads instantly. Every entry is a placeholder until the owner supplies real ones; nothing invented. Owner rejected the Maker's Tags direction as too busy.
Memorable moment: a page as calm and exact as a hand press's colophon, with one red ampersand.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a colophon, a plain record of what one person made and how to reach them, set with a printer's care. It refuses both the card grid and decorative busyness.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white book paper and black ink, with one spot red kept for the ampersand mark and the section labels. One face, Libre Caslon, in roman, italic and letterspaced small caps. Hairline rules only where a printer would put them. No boxes, shadows or icons.

STORY: The visitor sees who (one person, one studio), then what: a single dense ruled band listing Products, Services and Experiments. They follow a link or write.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Generous white. A red ampersand mark about 5rem high, the name in spaced small caps under it, and one italic sentence. The ruled band starts above the fold, with red small-caps labels hanging in a left margin column and entries in the main column.

FORM: The Colophon, candidate 4 of 7 on the re-rolled list, seed key 0d98511f (re-roll 1). Raises: one dense band against a bare page (obi); empty slots drawn on purpose (seven-segment). Signature interaction: a link's rule draws in red under it on hover or focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
